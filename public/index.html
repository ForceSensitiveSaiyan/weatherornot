    How it works

    Every day there are 3 calls on tomorrow's weather where you are. Change your mind as often as you like until midnight.

    10 pts for each right call · +10 if you called it against the forecast · +5 if a third or fewer of the players agreed with you.

    Results land the morning after. Free to play: no money, just bragging rights.
