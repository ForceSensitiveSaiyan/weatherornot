    How it works

    Three yes or no questions about tomorrow's weather where you live. You can change your answers until midnight.

    Rain: the less likely answer scores more, and the points are on the buttons. Temperature and wind: 10 for a right answer. Tap ★ to double one answer a day. If the reading lands exactly on the number asked, everyone who answered gets 5.

    Each question is set right by the forecast, adjusted for how forecasts usually run in your town, so just copying the forecast is a coin flip. Next morning we check the nearest official weather station and post the results.

    It's free. No money, just bragging rights.
