Privacy

WeatherOrNot is a free game. We keep as little about you as we can, and we never sell it or show you ads.

What we keep

  • Your player name. We make one up when you first answer (like BreezyOtter42). If you save your account, you choose your own name and a password. We only keep a scrambled version of the password (a hash), never the password itself.
  • Your answers and scores, the towns you play, your streak, and the leagues you join. Other players see your name and points on leaderboards and league tables.
  • Anything you send us through "Something look wrong?".
  • A login cookie, so the site remembers you. It's essential for the game to work, so we don't ask about it. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Your internet address (IP), held briefly in memory to stop people abusing sign-ups and logins. We don't store it.

Your chosen town is also saved in your browser, so the game opens on it next time.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing your data.
  • We never ask for your device's location. We only look at its time-zone setting, to suggest a town (an Isle of Man time zone suggests Douglas). You pick your town yourself.
  • Weather data is fetched by our server, so weather services never see your device.

Why we keep it

To run the game you asked to play (UK GDPR: performing our agreement with you), and to keep it fair and safe (our legitimate interests).

How long

While you play. Ask us to delete your account and we'll remove your name, answers and scores.
